Romeo With A Dumbbell

A workout ledger for the iPhone.

Romeo With A Dumbbell is a log for strength training. You write down what you lift — the exercise, the weight, the repetitions, how hard it felt — and the app keeps the ledger: every session, every set, read and computed on the phone, with or without a connection.

What it does

  • Log. Templates, supersets, a rest timer, and a suggested weight and repetitions for each set, worked out from what you lifted last time. Kilos or pounds.
  • Routine. Plan the week. The app says what was done, what was missed, and what comes next.
  • History. Every session by day, and a chart of sets over time for any muscle.
  • Insights. Observations about your training, from your own numbers. Silence is a feature: they say nothing until they have earned the right to.
  • Periodisation. Training blocks with a deload at the end, and a generated programme for someone who needs one.
  • Journal. Pages of your own, written freely or under a prompt, locked with Face ID if you like.
  • Import. Bring your history in from Strong, Hevy and other apps’ exports, and take it out again as a CSV file at any time.
  • Backup. An account exists for one reason: your workouts back up to your own private folder after every save. Body measurements only if you say so.
  • Nothing else. No ads. Nothing sold. Nothing tracked across other apps or sites.
