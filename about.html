The company behind the app

Gentagelse Ltd develops and publishes Romeo With A Dumbbell, a workout ledger for the iPhone, and runs the service that backs up its users’ training. The app is the company’s work; there is no other product.

Support

If something in the app is wrong, or you have a question about your account or your data, write to contact@romeolifts.com. It helps to say which version of the app you are using and which iPhone it is on. You can also send a message from inside the app, under Profile.

Your information

Gentagelse Ltd is the data controller for the information the app collects. Our Privacy Policy sets out what that is, why, where it is kept and the choices you have, and our Terms of Use govern your use of the app. Requests about your personal information go to the same address, and we reply within one month.
