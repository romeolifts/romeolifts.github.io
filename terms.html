Strength training carries a risk of injury. The app does not give medical advice, and it cannot see you, your technique or your health.

Speak to a doctor before starting or changing a training programme, especially if you have a medical condition or an injury, are pregnant, or have not trained for some time. Stop at once and seek advice if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or discomfort in your chest. You decide what to lift: use your own judgement, warm up properly, and use a spotter or safety equipment where appropriate. If you use the breathing exercises, practise them seated, never while driving or in water, and stop if you feel light-headed. If you have a heart or lung condition, speak to a doctor before using them.
