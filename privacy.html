Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

The short version

  • Everything you record is kept on your phone, and the app works without a connection.
  • An account is required, and it exists for one reason: backup. Your workouts go to your own private folder on the server after every save. Your body measurements go only if you tick the box for them.
  • If you send feedback from the app, it is kept with your account.
  • The app can send anonymous counts of which screens and features get used — never a weight, a rep, a measurement or anything you type. In the EU, the EEA and Switzerland you are asked first; anywhere, it is off at one tap.
  • Nothing is sold. There are no ads, and nothing tracks you across other apps or websites.
  • Delete your account in the app and everything on the server is deleted with it.

1. Who we are

Romeo With A Dumbbell (“the app”) is made by Romeo With A Dumbbell, contact@romeolifts.com — “we”, “us” and “our” in this policy. We are the controller of the personal data described here under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and, for people in the EU and EEA, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Questions, requests and complaints: contact@romeolifts.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The app is built to work offline. Logging, history, insights, the body map, programmes and every calculation happen on the phone. These are kept only on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • the name you give yourself in the profile;
  • templates, routines, programmes and training blocks;
  • exercises and activities you create, and your notes on machine set-up, rest and equipment;
  • your settings — units, appearance, and the rest;
  • your body measurements, unless you turn on their backup (section 3.2);
  • anything in the breathing screen.

Like any app’s data, these may be included in your phone’s own backups to iCloud or a computer, if you have those turned on. That is between you and Apple, under Apple’s terms.

You can use the app without an account (Start training). Until you sign up, nothing at all leaves the phone — not even usage counts.

3. What leaves your phone, and why

3.1 Your account

To sign up or sign in you give an email address. The app emails you a six-digit code; nobody has a password. If you choose Continue with Google, Google confirms your email address to the sign-in service and also passes on the name, profile picture link and account ID from your Google account; these are stored with your account record, and the app does not use them. If the app offers Sign in with Apple and you use it, Apple passes on your email address, or a private relay address if you choose to hide yours.

Your account is an email address and a random user ID. The sign-in service also keeps short-lived security logs of sign-in requests, which include the IP address and the device’s user agent.

3.2 Your backup

Workouts, always. After every saved session, after an import and whenever a backup is owed, the app uploads your workouts: for each one, its date, start and end times and time zone, its name and any notes you wrote on it, every exercise, cardio and stretch with its sets — weight, reps, distance and time — and the tags, muscles and modes the app records with them.

Body measurements, only if you choose. If you tick Also back up my body measurements when you sign up or sign in, or turn on Body measurements backup in Profile › Your data, the backup also carries your weight, body fat, measurements and training phase. This is information about your health, and the app backs it up only with your explicit consent. Turn it off and it is deleted from every stored snapshot at the next backup.

Nothing else. No templates, routines, blocks, custom exercises, settings, and never your name.

The backup is kept as compressed snapshots: one for each of the last seven days you backed up and one for each of the last four weeks. Older snapshots are deleted automatically. Alongside them the server keeps one small record per account: when you last backed up, which snapshots are kept, and whether body measurements are included. Your folder can be read only by your signed-in account; the database enforces that, not the app.

Before each upload the app reads your latest snapshot and merges it into the phone, so that a second phone on the same account never overwrites the first.

3.3 Feedback

When you tap Send on Say it to my face, the subject and message you wrote are stored with your account and the time they were sent, so that we can read them and reply. You can send up to five a day. You cannot read them back in the app, and they are deleted with your account.

3.4 Anonymous usage counts

If sharing is on, the app sends counts of how it is used, so we can fix what doesn’t work. Each event is one of a fixed list: a screen shown (Body, Log, History and so on); the app opened; and actions such as session started, session saved, template loaded, exercise added, rest started, programme generated or setting changed — with at most one word about it from a fixed list (for example, whether a session was saved by hand or by auto-save, or which setting changed to which choice).

Each event also carries the time, the app’s version and build, the region set in your phone’s Settings (two letters, such as GB), and a random install ID.

  • Never sent: a weight, a rep, a count, a measurement, which measurement, which training phase, an exercise’s name, or anything you type. No device model, operating system or advertising identifier.
  • Not linked to you. The install ID is made by the app, is never joined to your account, and is replaced with a new one when you turn sharing off, when you sign out and when you delete your account. The region is your setting, not your location; no location is looked up from your IP address, and the analytics service is set to discard IP addresses.
  • Your choice. In the EU, the EEA and Switzerland, sharing is off unless you tick the box when you sign up or sign in. Everywhere else it starts on when you agree to the Terms. Anywhere, Profile › About › Share usage statistics turns it off; nothing is sent or stored while it is off.

5. Who processes it for us

We use a small number of providers, each under a data processing agreement and only to run the parts of the app described here:

  • Supabase — accounts, sign-in, the backup and feedback. Your data is stored in London, United Kingdom.
  • Resend — sends the sign-in code emails, so it receives your email address and the code. Resend is based in the United States.
  • PostHog — receives the anonymous usage counts, on its EU cloud in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Google — only if you choose Continue with Google, under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Apple — distributes the app through the App Store, under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Where a provider handles data outside the UK and the EEA, the transfer is protected by the safeguards the law requires, such as adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, and only as far as it requires.

6. How long it is kept

  • Account, backup record and snapshots: until you delete your account. Snapshots roll — seven daily and four weekly — so older copies go on their own.
  • Body measurements in the backup: until you turn their backup off or delete your account.
  • Feedback: until you delete your account.
  • Security logs: a few days.
  • Usage counts: up to one year, then deleted.

7. Signing out and deleting your account

Signing out (Profile › Your data › Sign out) backs up first, then clears your workouts, measurements and name from the phone. Your workouts, and your measurements if their backup is on, come back when you sign in again; your name does not, because it was never uploaded. If the backup cannot be made, nothing is cleared.

Deleting your account (Profile › Your data › Delete account) removes every snapshot, then your account, its backup record and your feedback from the server. What is on your phone stays on it; delete the app to remove that too. You can also ask us by email to delete your account.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how it is used, to take a copy of it in a portable form, and to withdraw your consent at any time — without affecting what was done before you withdrew it.

  • A copy: Profile › Your data › Export CSV gives you your workouts and measurements as files you keep. You can also ask us for what the server holds.
  • Deletion: Delete account, in the app, at any time.
  • Consent: turn off Body measurements backup or Share usage statistics.

Because usage counts are never linked to you, we cannot find the counts that came from your phone; turning sharing off starts a new ID that cannot be joined to the old one.

Write to contact@romeolifts.com for anything else; we will answer within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) in the UK, or to the data protection authority where you live in the EU or EEA.

9. Security

Everything sent between the app and the server travels encrypted over HTTPS. Each account’s backup folder and record can be read only by that account, enforced by the database’s own row-level rules. Your sign-in session is kept in the iPhone’s Keychain. There are no passwords to leak. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affects your data we will tell you, and the regulator, as the law requires.

10. Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 16. If you are under 16, do not create an account. If you believe a child has, write to us and we will delete it.

11. This website

romeolifts.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

12. Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you before it takes effect.
